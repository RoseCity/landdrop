## What it is

LandDrop is a place to practise Commander. Pick a deck, sit down with computer opponents and play a full game, with the rules handled for you by [Forge](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge)’s rules engine. The table is built to be easy to read: you can always see what’s on the stack, what’s attacking what, and why the game is asking you something.

It’s for learning your cards, trying out a new deck, or just getting a game in when your pod isn’t around.

## Features

- **Decks:** 180 ready-made Commander decks to start with, or import your own from Moxfield, Archidekt, TappedOut, MTGGoldfish or a pasted list.
- **Opponents:** Forge’s computer players. Free, and they work offline.
- **A readable table:** the stack laid out across the table, drag to attack and block, clear prompts, and a game log with card previews.
- **The Lab:** set up any board you like, try combos from Commander Spellbook, and walk through 80+ mechanics step by step.
- **Puzzles** to solve, **Rewind** to replay a turn differently, and **Save & leave** to finish a game later.
- **Deck insights:** brackets, salt, mana curve, combos in your deck, and your own play statistics.
- **Other formats:** Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage and Pauper.
- **Make it yours:** card backs (animated GIFs too), playmats, colour-blind palettes, and your own music from YouTube or SoundCloud.
- **Optional AI coach:** explanations, a post-game review and AI-model opponents, with an API key of your own. You don’t need it to play.

## Install

1. Click **Download LandDrop** above.
2. Run `LandDrop-Setup.exe`. LandDrop is a free hobby project and isn’t signed, so Windows may say *“Windows protected your PC”*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Start **LandDrop** from the Start menu. The first start takes a minute or two (it loads 34,000 cards); later starts are quicker.

That’s it: Java comes with it, nothing else to install. When a new version is out, the main menu shows **Update**: one click and it installs itself, keeping your decks and settings.

## Your data

Your decks, settings and games stay on your PC. LandDrop only goes online for card pictures and deck lookups (Scryfall, Commander Spellbook, EDHREC, the deck sites you import from), to check for updates, and to reach your AI provider if you add a key.
